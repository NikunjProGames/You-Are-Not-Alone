A house with a history.

YOU ARE NOT ALONE is a first-person psychological horror game about finding an affordable place to live, learning the rhythms of a shared home, and deciding what to believe as small details stop adding up.

Explore, observe, and speak with the people who live there. Episode One is playable free in your browser.
