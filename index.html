A place to start again.

Sometimes, the cheapest place you can find turns out to be the one you should have avoided. After an unexpected loss and financial trouble leave you searching for a fresh start, an unusually affordable house seems like a chance to begin again. For a while, things feel as though they might finally work out.

Then the little things start to feel different. YOU ARE NOT ALONE is a story-driven psychological horror game about an ordinary living situation that slowly becomes deeply unsettling. Strange events, uncomfortable conversations, and suspicious behaviour raise questions about the people around you. The more you explore, the harder it becomes to tell who can be trusted.

Explore a mysterious house, look around carefully, follow conversations, and pay attention to the details as the story unfolds. With blocky 3D visuals, an unsettling atmosphere, and a mystery to piece together, this indie horror adventure is made for players who enjoy psychological horror, story-based games, creepy houses, and atmospheric exploration. Play Episode One free in your browser, with no installation required.

You came looking for a place to start over. Now you have to find out what kind of place you have walked into. Would you still stay if you knew you weren't alone?

About the creator

YOU ARE NOT ALONE — A PleyZ Original — Created by Nikunj Arora

A browser game developer and designer, Nikunj Arora is passionate about creating immersive experiences that blend storytelling, gameplay, and technology. With a focus on psychological horror and adventure genres, he aims to craft games that challenge players' perceptions and evoke deep emotional responses. His work emphasizes accessibility, allowing players to engage with his creations directly in their web browsers without the need for installations or downloads.
